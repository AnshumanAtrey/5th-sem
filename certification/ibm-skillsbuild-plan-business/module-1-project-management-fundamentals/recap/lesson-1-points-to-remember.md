Points to remember

Key concepts

1

A project is a temporary endeavor undertaken to produce a unique product, service, or result. A project has constraints and delivers value.

2

Projects focus on specific objectives, but they can be grouped into programs that align with broader business goals. These programs contribute to the overall strategy of a portfolio, helping organizations deliver value.

3

Project management is the process of planning, organizing, and managing the execution of a project to achieve specific goals within a set timeline, budget, and scope.

4

Effective project management provides value to organizations by:

Offering clarity and guidance

Ensuring that resources are efficiently used

Highlighting risks and identifying mitigation plans

Improving collaboration and communication within a team

Specifying a project’s success criteria

5

By understanding the potential challenges to a project, organizations can avoid the project’s failure and increase the likelihood of its success.
