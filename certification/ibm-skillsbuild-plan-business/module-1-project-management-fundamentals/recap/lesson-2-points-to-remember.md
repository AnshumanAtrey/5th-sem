Points to remember

Key concepts

1

Project managers are responsible for planning, executing, and overseeing a project to ensure the team successfully completes the project. They coordinate the team, manage resources, and monitor progress to achieve specific goals within a set time frame and budget.

2

The following are the key responsibilities of PMs:

Define project goals and scope

Develop and maintain plans

Allocate and oversee tasks

Ensure timely delivery

Manage changes and risks

Communicate with stakeholders

Lead and motivate teams

3

To maintain a team’s morale and deliver results, PMs must have the following competencies:

Leadership

Analytical thinking

Communication

Adaptability

Time management

Collaboration and teaming

Decision making

Problem solving

4

Stakeholders are individuals, groups, or organizations who have an interest in or are affected by the outcome of a project. Stakeholders can influence the project or be influenced by its results.

5

Based on their level of involvement, there are two types of stakeholders: internal and external.

6

Different stakeholders have different interests.

Internal stakeholders might focus on organizational growth, efficient use of resources, and career development.

External stakeholders might focus on quality and performance, financial gains, legal and safety requirements, fair and consistent terms, and business relationships.

7

The following are the risks associated with overlooking or mismanaging stakeholders’ interests:

Employee disengagement and low morale

Miscommunication and unclear expectations

Conflicting interests

Resistance to change

Customer dissatisfaction and loss of business

Financial and investment risks

Compliance and legal consequences

To successfully navigate the responsibilities and risks, PMs must follow a structured approach. In the next lesson, you’ll explore the phases and frameworks of project management.
