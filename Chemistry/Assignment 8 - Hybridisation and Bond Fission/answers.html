Question. Explain the types of hybridisation with examples. Differentiate homolytic vs heterolytic fission.

Hybridisation = mixing of atomic orbitals of nearly equal energy into equivalent hybrids.

  • sp3: 1s+3p → 4 hybrids, tetrahedral, 109.5°, 4 σ (methane, ethane)
  • sp2: 1s+2p → 3 hybrids, trigonal planar, 120°, 3 σ + 1 π (ethene, benzene)
  • sp: 1s+1p → 2 hybrids, linear, 180°, 2 σ + 2 π (ethyne, CO2)
