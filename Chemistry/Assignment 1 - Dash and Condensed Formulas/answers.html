Question. Write the dash (expanded structural) formula and the condensed formula for the following compounds: C2H6, C4H8, C3H4, C3H6, C5H10, C4H10.

Rule. The dash (expanded) formula shows every atom and every bond as a line; the condensed formula groups each H with its carbon and drops the C–H lines (= / # mark double / triple bonds).
