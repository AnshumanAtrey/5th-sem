Question. Explain the stability of a carbanion and of a free radical (inductive, resonance, alkyl/benzyl).

Carbanion: −I (electron-withdrawing) groups increase stability; +I alkyl groups destabilise, so the alkyl order is methyl > 1° > 2° > 3° (opposite to carbocation). Resonance strongly stabilises — allyl and benzyl carbanions delocalise into the π system.

Free radical: +I alkyl groups and hyperconjugation increase stability → 3° > 2° > 1° > methyl; resonance makes allyl and benzyl radicals especially stable.
