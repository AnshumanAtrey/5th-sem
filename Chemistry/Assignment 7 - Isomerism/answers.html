Question. What is isomerism? What is threo and erythro? Classify isomerism with examples.

Isomerism: compounds with the same molecular formula but a different arrangement of atoms (structure) or spatial orientation.

Structural (constitutional): chain, position, functional, metamerism, tautomerism. Stereoisomerism: geometrical (cis/trans, E/Z) and optical (R/S; enantiomers & diastereomers).

Threo / erythro describe the relative configuration of two adjacent stereocentres: erythro = like groups on the same side in the Fischer projection (meso-like); threo = like groups on opposite sides (the (±) pair).
